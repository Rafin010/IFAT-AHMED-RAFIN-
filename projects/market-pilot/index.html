1. Problem & Context

Modern financial traders face extreme data overload. Fragmented price action, conflicting social sentiment, and rapid liquidity shifts across centralized and decentralized exchanges make objective decision-making nearly impossible without automated quantitative telemetry.

Market Pilot was designed to consolidate dispersed market variables into a single high-fidelity, real-time dashboard powered by machine learning heuristics.

3. Key Innovations

  • Composite Order Flow Analysis: Aggregating market depth from multiple liquidity venues to detect hidden institutional iceberg orders.
  • Real-Time Sentiment Vectors: Transformer-based NLP pipelines score macro financial news and social media chatter in under 120ms.
  • Zero-Lag Canvas Rendering: Using hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas to plot thousands of tick candles without degrading browser frame rates.
